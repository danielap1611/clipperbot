ClipperBot

A personal desktop tool for clipping stream highlights

What it does

  • Runs on the owner's own Windows PC.
  • Watches the owner's chosen Twitch/Kick streams or videos and finds highlight moments (loud reactions, chat hype, big on-screen moments).
  • Cuts those moments into short vertical clips with captions.
  • With the owner's permission, sends finished clips to the owner's own TikTok account as drafts using TikTok's official Content Posting API. The owner reviews and posts them in the TikTok app.

Who it's for

ClipperBot is a private, non-commercial tool used only by its owner with their own TikTok account. It is not offered to the public and does not access anyone else's account.
