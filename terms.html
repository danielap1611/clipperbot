ClipperBot Terms of Service

Last updated: October 17, 2026

1. What ClipperBot is

ClipperBot is a personal, non-commercial desktop tool. It finds highlight moments in videos and live streams chosen by its owner, turns them into short clips, and can send those clips to the owner's own TikTok account as drafts.

2. Who may use it

ClipperBot is used only by its owner, on the owner's own computer, with the owner's own TikTok account. It is not offered or sold to anyone else.

3. Content and rights

The user is responsible for making sure they have the right to clip and share any content they process with ClipperBot, and for following the rules of Twitch, Kick, YouTube and TikTok, including TikTok's Terms of Service and Community Guidelines.

4. TikTok integration

ClipperBot connects to TikTok only through TikTok's official APIs and only after the user authorizes it on TikTok's own login page. It uploads clips to the user's TikTok inbox as drafts. Nothing is published until the user posts it in the TikTok app. The user can disconnect ClipperBot at any time in ClipperBot or in their TikTok settings.

5. No warranty

ClipperBot is provided "as is", without warranties of any kind. It may make mistakes in choosing or cutting clips, and the user should review clips before posting them.

6. Changes

These terms may be updated. The date at the top shows the latest version.
